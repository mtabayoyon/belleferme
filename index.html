Venue Layout

The garden is organized into connected event zones — tap the site plan to explore.

Ceremony Lawn

Open lawn framed by heritage oaks with a ceremony arbor — seats rows of guests with a cocktail terrace alongside.

Reception Lawn

Market-lit lawn sized for round-table dining and dancing under the string lights.

Dining Pavilion

Covered pavilion with long farm tables — dinner service rain or shine.

Bar & Cocktail Terrace

Dedicated bar area adjacent to the reception lawn, plus a cocktail terrace off the ceremony space.

Lounge & Fire Pit

Soft-seating lounge around the fire pit for golden-hour conversation.

Grove Walk & Gardens

Lit orchard grove walk and native plantings along the swale — a natural backdrop for photos.

The Barn

Flexible indoor event space for prep, staging, or weather backup.
